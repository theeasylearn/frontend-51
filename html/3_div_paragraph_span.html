The Little Friends in Sunny Meadow

Once upon a time, in a bright green place called Sunny Meadow, five little animal friends lived happily together.
There was **Rory the Rabbit**, who loved to hop and laugh. He had soft white fur and very long ears. Rory liked to smell flowers and play hide-and-seek.
Next was **Ellie the Elephant**. Ellie was big, but very gentle. She used her long trunk to splash water and help her friends. Everyone felt safe near Ellie.
Then there was **Timmy the Turtle**. Timmy was slow, but very smart. He carried his little green shell everywhere and loved sunny naps. Timmy always said, “Slow is okay.”
**Milo the Monkey** lived in a tall tree. He liked to swing, jump, and make funny faces. Milo made everyone laugh with his silly tricks.
Up in the sky flew **Bella the Bird**. She had bright blue feathers and sang sweet songs every morning. Bella could see everything from above and loved to help.
One day, Rory lost his favorite red ball. He felt sad. All the friends decided to help.
Ellie looked near the pond. Timmy checked behind the rocks. Milo jumped from tree to tree. Bella flew high and looked everywhere.
Finally, Bella chirped, “I see it!” The ball was stuck near a bush. Ellie gently pulled it out with her trunk.
Rory smiled and hugged his friends.
They all clapped, laughed, and played together until the sun went down.
And every night after that, the five friends remembered one thing—**helping and sharing make hearts happy**. 🌈🧸

The Five Friends at Rainbow Pond

Once upon a time, near a shiny blue pond called Rainbow Pond, five little animal friends lived together. They loved to play, laugh, and help each other every day.
There was **Benny the Bear**, who loved warm hugs and sweet honey. Benny was fluffy and kind. He liked to sit in the sun and smile at his friends.
Next came **Lulu the Lion**. Lulu was small but brave. She had a soft golden mane and a gentle roar. Lulu liked to run and protect her friends.
Then there was **Penny the Penguin**. Penny walked with a cute waddle and loved cold water. She liked to splash and slide, making everyone giggle.
**Ollie the Owl** lived in a quiet tree. He had big round eyes and fluffy wings. Ollie liked to watch at night and say wise, happy words.
Near the pond hopped **Freddy the Frog**. Freddy was green and jumpy. He loved to say “Ribbit!” and jump very high.
One sunny morning, Penny lost her shiny blue pebble. She felt sad and quiet. All her friends wanted to help.
Benny looked near the flowers. Lulu ran around the grass. Freddy jumped by the pond. Ollie looked from the tree.
Suddenly, Freddy croaked, “Ribbit! I found it!” The pebble was near a lily pad.
Penny was so happy. She smiled and clapped her flippers.
The friends played together all day and shared snacks by the pond.
And they all learned one sweet thing—**friends who help are friends forever**. 🌈🐾

नन्हे दोस्तों की रंगीन ट्रेन

एक बार की बात है, एक रंगीन ट्रेन में पाँच प्यारे जानवर सफर कर रहे थे। ट्रेन बहुत धीरे-धीरे चल रही थी।
**चीकू चूहे** को चीज़ बहुत पसंद थी। वह खुश होकर “चूं-चूं” करता रहता था।
**गोलू गाय** सफेद और मुलायम थी। वह सबको प्यार से देखती और “मूं-मूं” कहती।
**पिंकू पांडा** आराम से बैठा था। उसे सोना और मुस्कुराना अच्छा लगता था।
**बिट्टू भालू** थोड़ा भारी था, लेकिन बहुत दोस्ताना था। वह सबको गले लगाना चाहता था।
**सोनू तोता** हरा-भरा और चतुर था। वह नए-नए शब्द बोलता था।
अचानक ट्रेन रुक गई। सब चुप हो गए। सोनू ने ऊपर से देखा और बोला, “पटरी पर पत्ता है!”
बिट्टू ने पत्ता हटाया। ट्रेन फिर चल पड़ी।
सब जानवर खुश हो गए और ताली बजाने लगे।
उस दिन सभी ने सीखा—**मिलकर काम करने से हर समस्या आसान हो जाती है**। 🚆😊

પાંચ મિત્રો અને મીઠું ફળ

એક વખત એક લીલા જંગલમાં પાંચ નાનકડા પશુ મિત્રો રહેતા હતા. તેઓ હંમેશા સાથે રમતા.
**લાલુ સસલું** ખૂબ જ ઝડપથી દોડતું અને હસતું.
**મિનુ બિલાડી** નરમ અને શાંત હતી. તેને દૂધ પીવું ગમતું.
**કાનુ કૂતરો** વફાદાર હતો અને બધાની રક્ષા કરતો.
**ટોટો બતક** પાણીમાં છપછપ કરતો અને મજા કરતો.
**પિહુ ચકલી** નાની હતી પરંતુ ખૂબ ચપળ હતી.
એક દિવસ તેમને એક મીઠું ફળ મળ્યું, પણ ફળ એક જ હતું. બધા વિચારમાં પડી ગયા.
પિહુ બોલી, “ચાલો વહેંચી લઈએ.”
મિનુએ ફળ કાપ્યું, લાલુએ ભાગ પાડ્યા, અને બધાએ સાથે ખાધું.
બધા ખુશ થયા અને હસ્યા.
તે દિવસ પછી બધા સમજ્યા—**વહેંચવાથી ખુશી વધે છે**। 🍎🐾
